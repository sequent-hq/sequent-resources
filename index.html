06 · HELP

Help

ONE-CLICK PUBLIC LINKS

Every link on this board is a public share page — no account needed, safe to forward to partners, vendors, and press. If a link ever says the collection isn't available, its Publish online toggle got switched off in the portal — flip it back on and the link works again.

DOWNLOADING FILES

Links open the file inside its collection. Select it there and hit Download for the original, full-resolution version. Players on this board stream 720p previews; full-res 4K files run 0.5–2.3 GB — download on wifi.

CAN'T FIND IT?

Search everything at brand.kreate.com ↗ (team sign-in). Need something made, or a new collection published? Ask in the marketing channel and it gets added to this board.
